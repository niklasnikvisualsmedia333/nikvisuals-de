# NikVisuals product brief

NikVisuals by Niklas Brüne is a founder-led business/portfolio brand evolving from professional media production toward selected AI, Growth and Marketing projects for ambitious B2B companies. The website is a digital business card, not a large SEO or lead-generation site. Keep it readable in approximately three minutes and under five minutes overall.

Primary headline: “AI, Growth & Marketing für ambitionierte B2B-Unternehmen.” Primary action: “Projekt besprechen” via info@nikvisuals.de. Secondary: “Arbeiten ansehen”.

## Page architecture

Minimal header → founder-led solo-image hero → three expandable current areas → short study/practice bridge and compact timeline → collaboration signal → projects grouped as Business, Workshops & Community and Media & Production → compact review proof → personal About → email-preparing contact form and direct email → minimal footer. German primary, concise English equivalent with real static output at `/en/`.

Current areas: AI & Prozesse; Growth, GTM & Business Development; Marketing & Content. Existing tools, research and pragmatic execution define the scope; no custom software agency positioning.

## Credibility and claim boundaries

The main source for client/project and background statements is the owner's supplied relaunch brief. Most earlier work is media, content and workshops; selected strategic/Business Development experience is separately identified. Core verified cases: LapStore (content plus later Business Development), SMS group (Corporate Media), Stadt Hilchenbach (Content & Distribution), IHK Siegen (Workshops). A category and factual short description accompany every case. No logos next to AI/Growth positioning and no fabricated impact claims.

Professional independent work since 2021, master’s studies in Entrepreneurship & SME Management at Universität Siegen, academic AI/digital-marketing work and general practical AI/process experience explain the broader perspective. No claim that the master's degree is completed. About uses first person; no implied large team. Confidential work remains general and unidentifiable.

## Visual direction

Warm cream, deep ink, electric blue and soft blue, large tight self-hosted Manrope type and alternating high-contrast modules. The Hero uses one of three owner-supplied solo founder stills selected via `?hero=1`, `?hero=2` or `?hero=3`. Dark is the default; light remains a saved visitor choice. About and `/links/` use the current clean profile portrait. A muted responsive background loop appears only in Media & Production, after it approaches the viewport. The video archive holds 33 audited, click-only YouTube cards with local thumbnails, factual categories and DE/EN summaries. Tech Meets Problems is a community-project reference, not an offer. Review cards each show five stars in a compact horizontally scrollable row, with project context. No stock/AI illustration and lightweight CSS interactions only. See `DESIGN_REFERENCE.md`.

## Copy standard

Visible copy is direct, concrete and short. Prefer factual wording such as “Selbstständig seit 2021.” Avoid generic AI/consulting slogans and invented brand statements. `/links/` is a functional link hub, not a marketing page. Remove visual or content repetition rather than adding volume. Customer feedback is paraphrased earlier proof, never evidence for current AI services; reverify the Google count and rating before production launch.

The contact form validates in the browser only and never sends, stores or claims to have sent data. Its status message tells visitors to use the direct email fallback until an approved endpoint exists.

## Preview boundary

GitHub Pages project URL only. Always noindex. No DNS, Wix, mail, production-domain or CNAME changes. Separate production authorization is required later.

# NikVisuals product brief

NikVisuals by Niklas Brüne is a founder-led business/portfolio brand evolving from professional media production toward selected AI, Growth and Marketing projects for ambitious B2B companies. The website is a digital business card, not a large SEO or lead-generation site. Keep it readable in approximately three minutes and under five minutes overall.

Primary headline: “AI, Growth & Marketing für ambitionierte B2B-Unternehmen.” Primary action: “Projekt besprechen” via info@nikvisuals.de. Secondary: “Arbeiten ansehen”.

## Page architecture

Minimal header → media-led hero → three compact current areas → short study/practice bridge → four previous projects plus explicitly-qualified collaboration strip → personal About → frontend-only contact form and direct email → minimal footer. German primary, concise English equivalent with real static output at `/en/`. No extra case pages, service packages, timeline, showreel, testimonial or press wall.

Current areas: AI & Prozesse; Growth, GTM & Business Development; Marketing & Content. Existing tools, research and pragmatic execution define the scope; no custom software agency positioning.

## Credibility and claim boundaries

The main source for client/project and background statements is the owner's supplied relaunch brief. Most historical work is media, content and workshops; selected strategic/Business Development experience is separately identified. Four cases only: LapStore (content plus later Business Development), SMS group (Corporate Media), Stadt Hilchenbach (Content & Distribution), IHK Siegen (Workshops). A category and factual short description accompany every case. No logos next to AI/Growth positioning and no fabricated impact claims.

Professional independent work since 2021, master’s studies in Entrepreneurship & SME Management at Universität Siegen, current Business Exchange at University of Tulsa, academic AI/digital-marketing work and general practical AI/process experience explain the broader perspective. No claim that the master's degree is completed. About uses first person; no implied large team. Confidential work remains general and unidentifiable.

## Visual direction

Keravonos-inspired but distinct visual energy: warm cream, deep ink, electric blue, acid-lime details, large tight local grotesk type and alternating high-contrast modules. The hero uses an asymmetric collage from real NikVisuals material. Project media alternates with intentional typographic previews where verified imagery is missing. System fonts, local images, no stock/AI illustration and lightweight CSS interactions only. See `DESIGN_REFERENCE.md`.

The contact form validates in the browser only and never sends, stores or claims to have sent data. Its status message tells visitors to use the direct email fallback until an approved endpoint exists.

## Preview boundary

GitHub Pages project URL only. Always noindex. No DNS, Wix, mail, production-domain or CNAME changes. Separate production authorization is required later.
